A set is logged when it ends by failure or Relax. Stim and damage are session means with the change from that set in brackets. Stimulus saturates fast, so later sets add little to it while damage keeps climbing. Deficit 24h is the cumulative projection after that set. "Rest + refuel" keeps CNS fatigue, damage and stimulus totals so sets add up. Muscle B starts as a copy of A's fibres (fresh state) and is tuned independently. Sliders for fibres and fast-twitch share rebuild that muscle when released.

Illustrative, not predictive. This tool shows mechanisms: recruitment order, fatigue, set-to-set drop and recovery. It does not predict how much muscle you will grow. Each parameter below is tagged by where it comes from; most are guesses or fits to rep tables.

A set is logged when it ends by failure or Relax. Tension figures are session means per fibre, with the change from that set in brackets; an effective rep is one where the fibre spent at least half the rep above 30% of its max force. Damage is the session mean with the change from that set. Deficit 24h is the cumulative projection after that set. "Rest + refuel" keeps CNS fatigue, damage and tension totals so sets add up. Muscle B starts as a copy of A's fibres (fresh state) and is tuned independently. Sliders for fibres and fast-twitch share rebuild that muscle when released.
